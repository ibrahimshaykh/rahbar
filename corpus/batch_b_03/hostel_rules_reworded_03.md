## 1.1 Eligibility and Allocation

Hostel rooms are allocated by the Hostel Office to enrolled students in order of application date. First-year students from outside the city receive priority. Each room is shared by two students, and a request to change rooms can be filed only following the first four weeks of a semester. The Hostel Office is located on the ground floor of Block A and is open from 09:00 to 17:00 on working days.

## 1.2 Fees and Security Deposit

Every resident pays a refundable security deposit of Rs 15,000 at the time of allocation. The deposit is returned within 30 days of check-out, provided the room inventory is complete and no harm is recorded. Monthly hostel dues must be paid by the 10th of each month. A late payment attracts a surcharge of Rs 200 per week until the dues are cleared.

## 1.3 Curfew and Guests

Residents must be inside the hostel by 22:30 on weekdays. On Fridays and Saturdays the curfew is extended to 23:30. A occupant who returns following curfew without permission pays a fine of Rs 500 and is recorded by the night warden. Guests can visit the common chamber only, between 14:00 and 18:00, and solely with prior approval from the Warden. Overnight guests are not permitted under any circumstances.



## 1.4 Room Care and Inspections

Residents are accountable for the cleanliness of their rooms. The Warden inspects rooms every second Monday, and residents are informed on the notice board at least one day before. Electric heaters, immersion rods and cooking appliances are banned because they overload the wiring. Any appliance discovered during an inspection is confiscated and returned solely at check-out. Damage to furniture or fittings is billed to the occupants in equal shares unless one occupant accepts responsibility in writing.

## 1.5 Conduct and Complaints

Residents shall keep noise to a minimum between 22:00 and 07:00. Smoking is forbidden on the entire hostel premises. A complaint about conduct, maintenance or safety should be recorded on the complaint form available at the Hostel Office and handed to the Warden. The Warden must acknowledge the complaint within 2 working days and resolve it, or explain the delay, inside 7 working days. Unresolved complaints can be escalated to the Dean of Student Affairs.

## 1.6 Check-out

A occupant must vacate the chamber within 3 days following the last examination of the semester unless a summer stay has been approved. At check-out the Warden signs the inventory sheet. The security deposit is released solely after this signature has been recorded in the hostel ledger.

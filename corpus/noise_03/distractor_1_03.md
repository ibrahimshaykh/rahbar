## 1.1 Opening Hours

The main cafeteria opens at 08:00 and closes at 20:00 from Monday to Saturday. On Sundays only the snack counter operates, from 10:00 to 16:00. During the month of Ramadan the evening hours are shifted to begin after sunset.

## 1.2 Menu and Pricing

A standard lunch plate with rice, curry and salad costs Rs 450. A vegetarian plate costs Rs 380. Tea is Rs 60 and a bottle of chilled water is Rs 80. Weekly specials are posted on the notice board near the counter every Monday morning.

## 1.3 Tray Return Rules

Trays and cutlery must be returned to the washing window after every meal. Customers who leave trays on tables three times in one month lose the right to use the loyalty card for two weeks. The loyalty card gives a discount of 5 percent after ten plates.

## 1.4 Complaints

Comments about food quality are collected in the red box beside the cashier. The cafeteria supervisor reads the box every Friday and answers within 7 days on the same notice board.

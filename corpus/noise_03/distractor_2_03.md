## 2.1 Eligibility for a Parking Permit

Motorcycle and car permits are issued by the Security Office to staff and to senior students who hold a valid driving licence. A permit is valid for one calendar year and is linked to a single registration plate.

## 2.2 Application Steps

The applicant brings a copy of the vehicle registration book, a copy of the driving licence and two passport photographs to the Security Office. The processing time is 3 working days. A motorcycle permit costs Rs 1,500 and a car permit costs Rs 4,000 per year.

## 2.3 Parking Zones

Zone A near the main gate is reserved for staff. Zone B behind the sports ground is open to students. Parking on the lawn or in front of the fire exit is prohibited, and a vehicle parked there may be clamped.

## 2.4 Violations

A first violation brings a written warning. A second violation within a year costs Rs 2,000 as a fine. After a third violation the permit is cancelled for the rest of the year.

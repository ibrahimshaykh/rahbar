## 3.1 Dates and Venue

The annual sports gala takes place from 2 March to 6 March at the main sports ground. The opening ceremony starts at 10:00 on the first day, and the closing ceremony with the prize distribution starts at 17:00 on the last day.

## 3.2 Events

The gala includes cricket, football, badminton, table tennis, a 100 metre sprint and a tug of war. Each team has at most 12 registered players. Mixed teams are allowed only in badminton and table tennis.

## 3.3 Registration

Team captains register their teams at the Sports Office by 15 February. The registration form must carry the signatures of all players. A team that withdraws after the draw is announced loses its right to register in the next gala.

## 3.4 Prizes

The winning team receives a trophy and a certificate for each member. The runner-up team receives certificates only. The player of the tournament is announced by the referee panel after the final match.
